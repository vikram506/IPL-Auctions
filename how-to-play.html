How to Play

From zero to hammer in five minutes.

1 · Host a room

Click Host a Room, enter your name, choose Mega (₹120 Cr purse) or Mini (₹40 Cr), set the pace, and pick your franchise. You'll get a 6-character room code like K7QM2P.

2 · Friends join

Everyone opens the site and taps Join with Code, enters the code and their name, and claims any free franchise. The lobby shows who's claimed what. The host starts the auction when ready.

3 · Bidding

Players come to the block one at a time — top-rated marquee names first. The opening bid is the base price. Every new bid resets the hammer: it falls about 2.4 seconds after the last bid (pace dependent). If nobody bids by the clock, the player is unsold and returns in round two.

4 · Rules the server enforces

Squad of 16–25 · max 8 overseas · you can never bid more than your purse minus ₹40 L for each remaining empty slot. Bid ladder follows the real IPL: +₹5 L under 1 Cr, +₹10 L to 2 Cr, +₹20 L to 5 Cr, +₹40 L to 10 Cr, +₹80 L above.

5 · Admin ratings control 🔐

The admin can log in and change any player's rating or base price — even mid-auction. New ratings apply to every room instantly. Want Vaibhav Suryavanshi at 95? Done. Admin can also add brand-new players to the pool.

Strategy tips

💡 Set a ceiling per player before bidding starts — and stick to it.
💡 Watch the overseas counter: burning slots on 74-rated foreigners starves your XI later.
💡 Let rivals fight over players you value below market. There are 190+ names; you need 16.
💡 Round two is where squads get completed cheap — save ₹15–20 Cr for it.
